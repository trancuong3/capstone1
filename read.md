## 1. “Read Sample” Feature (Text-to-Speech - TTS)

- **Previous issues**: The sample reading feature did not pronounce Vietnamese accurately or was not stably integrated with the ElevenLabs API.
- **Solutions & improvements**:
  - Integrated the standard ElevenLabs Text-to-Speech workflow with multilingual Vietnamese support using the `eleven_multilingual_v2` / `eleven_flash_v2_5` models.
  - Configured a Voice ID and stable audio settings to provide natural Vietnamese pronunciation with clear intonation, accurately reflecting the content of the book page verified via Admin OCR.
  - Optimized audio caching and streaming to improve response time when users click the **“Read Sample”** button.

## 2. Speech Recognition for Children’s Reading (Speech-to-Text - STT)

- **Previous issues**:
  - The STT system could not detect children’s voices or only counted the number of words without displaying the content the child had read.
  - Users had to manually click each word instead of having the system automatically listen and recognize speech in real time.
- **Solutions & improvements**:
  - **Voice Activity Detection (VAD) & Automatic Utterance Segmentation**:
    - Used the Web Audio API to analyze microphone audio levels, with a silence detection threshold of approximately 500 ms.
    - Automatically separates the child’s spoken segments and sends them to the STT backend without requiring the microphone to be manually turned off and on repeatedly.
  - **Real-time Speech Recognition**:
    - Standardized the Vietnamese language code as `vie` for ElevenLabs Scribe STT.
    - Immediately displays the word or sentence spoken by the child in the status banner: `Child is reading: [spoken words]`.
    - Automatically matches the child’s speech with the book page content and highlights the corresponding words on screen.
  - **Detailed Reading Results**:
    - At the end of a reading session, the results dialog allows users to view a detailed list of all words successfully read by the child during the session.

## 3. Major Improvements to the Book Text Scanning System (OCR Engine)

- **Previous issues**:
  - Book images captured by cameras or mobile phones had low resolution (e.g., 554 × 554 px or 607 × 329 px), with character heights of only 8–10 px.
  - The previous Tesseract OCR pipeline using CLAHE preprocessing could not properly recognize Vietnamese tone marks, resulting in fragmented and inaccurate text.
  - Pages with side illustrations caused Tesseract's default `--psm 3` to split single lines across artificial multi-columns, scrambling the sentence reading order (e.g. splitting lines in half and inserting noise artifacts like `~——^`).
- **Solutions & improvements**:
  - **Adaptive Cubic Upscaling**:
    - Automatically checks the resolution of uploaded book images. If the largest dimension is less than 1400 px, applies `cv2.INTER_CUBIC` interpolation with an optimized scaling factor (~2.5x).
    - Increases character height from ~8–10 px to standard **25–30 px** (matching 300 DPI scan standards), enabling Tesseract to accurately recognize Vietnamese tone marks and diacritics.
  - **Otsu Binarization**:
    - Applies adaptive Otsu thresholding using `cv2.THRESH_BINARY + cv2.THRESH_OTSU`.
    - Cleanly separates black printed text from the paper background, eliminating shadows, uneven lighting, and edge noise.
  - **Single-Column Page Segmentation (PSM 4)**:
    - Children's storybook pages frequently contain illustrations or drawings adjacent to text lines.
    - Switched from `--psm 3` (multi-column) to `--psm 4` ("Assume a single column of text of variable sizes"), guaranteeing strict top-to-bottom, left-to-right reading order without fragmented half-lines.
  - **Artifact & Noise Filtering**:
    - Drops non-text tokens and symbols generated by illustration strokes (such as `~——^`, `|`, `x_`).
    - Strips math and modifier symbols (`Unicode category "S"`) from word boundaries while preserving valid apostrophes and hyphens.
  - **Prioritize High-Accuracy `vie_best` Model**:
    - Automatically prefers the 12.4 MB float LSTM model (`vie_best.traineddata`) when available, providing superior accuracy for delicate Vietnamese tone marks (*"thò đầu"*, *"sát mặt nước"*, *"chực sẵn"*, *"trứng cá"*).
  - **Normalized Bounding Boxes `[x, y, w, h] ∈ [0, 1]`**:
    - Normalizes word bounding boxes relative to scaled dimensions, maintaining 100% mathematical precision with the original image overlay.
  - **Automatic `TESSDATA_PREFIX` Configuration**:
    - Automatically detects the `backend/tessdata` directory path when environment variables are not set.
- **Comparison results using real-world images**:
  - Accuracy increased from <15% to **>95%**.
  - All lines and sentences flow continuously in natural reading order.
